Pagefold privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Pagefold works entirely offline on your PC. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, files, or usage information. Your PDFs and images are processed on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Pagefold has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The only online interaction is the optional one-time purchase of Pagefold Pro, which is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's own privacy policy. Pagefold does not see your payment details.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
